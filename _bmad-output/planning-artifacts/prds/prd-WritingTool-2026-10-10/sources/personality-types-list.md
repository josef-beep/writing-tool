# Personality Types List

A practical list of common personality labels followed by constructed blend-types. The first entries are drawn from established temperament and trait language. The later entries are mixed archetypes created by combining common traits into readable profiles.

1. **Sanguine** — Social, upbeat, expressive, and energized by people.
2. **Choleric** — Strong-willed, forceful, competitive, and action-oriented.
3. **Phlegmatic** — Calm, steady, patient, and hard to provoke.
4. **Melancholic** — Thoughtful, serious, sensitive, and detail-focused.
5. **Average** — A balanced, typical mix of traits without strong extremes.
6. **Reserved** — Quiet, emotionally stable, and socially selective.
7. **Self-Centered** — Attention-seeking, impulsive, and less concerned with others.
8. **Role Model** — Reliable, cooperative, emotionally stable, and socially responsible.
9. **High Openness** — Curious, imaginative, and drawn to new ideas.
10. **High Conscientiousness** — Organized, disciplined, and dependable.
11. **High Extraversion** — Outgoing, energetic, and stimulated by interaction.
12. **High Agreeableness** — Kind, trusting, and inclined toward harmony.
13. **High Neuroticism** — Emotionally reactive, stress-sensitive, and prone to worry.
14. **Curious Organizer** — Creative and open-minded, but structured in execution.
15. **Quiet Organizer** — Reserved, orderly, and consistent in habits.
16. **Warm Strategist** — Caring toward people while thinking several steps ahead.
17. **Bold Mediator** — Confident in conflict but focused on resolution.
18. **Calm Explorer** — Relaxed, adaptable, and interested in new experiences.
19. **Intense Visionary** — Big-idea thinker with strong emotion and drive.
20. **Social Idealist** — People-focused and motivated by values and improvement.
21. **Guarded Analyst** — Careful, skeptical, and slow to trust weak evidence.
22. **Dutiful Helper** — Responsible, supportive, and motivated by service.
23. **Restless Achiever** — Ambitious and productive, but rarely fully settled.
24. **Steady Caregiver** — Dependable, patient, and consistently supportive.
25. **Practical Diplomat** — Cooperative and realistic, with a focus on workable peace.
26. **Reflective Builder** — Thoughtful, deliberate, and motivated to create lasting results.
27. **Driven Commander** — Decisive, ambitious, and comfortable leading under pressure.
28. **Gentle Traditionalist** — Kind, stable, and oriented toward trusted routines.
29. **Adventurous Connector** — Social, curious, and eager to build new relationships.
30. **Methodical Skeptic** — Systematic, cautious, and unconvinced without proof.
31. **Creative Loyalist** — Imaginative but deeply committed to chosen people or causes.
32. **Cautious Reformer** — Wants change, but prefers controlled and careful progress.
33. **High-Energy Planner** — Fast-moving and enthusiastic, yet strongly organized.
34. **Low-Key Harmonizer** — Quiet, agreeable, and good at reducing tension.
35. **Competitive Organizer** — Structured, goal-focused, and motivated by winning.
36. **Empathic Visionary** — Big-picture thinker who stays tuned to human impact.
37. **Reserved Perfectionist** — Private, exacting, and uncomfortable with loose standards.
38. **Charismatic Stabilizer** — Socially influential while remaining calm and grounding.
39. **Independent Thinker** — Self-directed, reflective, and resistant to group pressure.
40. **Sensitive Collaborator** — Cooperative and perceptive, but easily affected by friction.
41. **Disciplined Explorer** — Curious about novelty while maintaining strong self-control.
42. **Expressive Reformer** — Vocal, energetic, and motivated to improve broken systems.
43. **Peace-Seeking Planner** — Organized and future-focused, with low appetite for conflict.
44. **Tactical Caregiver** — Supportive, but practical and efficient in helping others.
45. **Bold Innovator** — Risk-tolerant, inventive, and comfortable moving first.
46. **Stoic Executor** — Emotionally contained, reliable, and focused on completion.
47. **Imaginative Realist** — Creative in thought, but grounded in constraints.
48. **Structured Idealist** — Guided by principles and committed to orderly implementation.
49. **Loyal Protector** — Devoted, watchful, and motivated to defend people or values.
50. **Adaptive Diplomat** — Flexible, socially aware, and skilled at adjusting across contexts.
51. **Abrasive Commander** — Dominating, impatient, and dismissive of weaker voices.
52. **Cold Opportunist** — Calculating, self-serving, and loyal only when useful.
53. **Anxious Controller** — Needs control constantly and reacts poorly to uncertainty.
54. **Bitter Traditionalist** — Clings to old ways and resents change in others.
55. **Paranoid Strategist** — Always scanning for betrayal, often without clear cause.
56. **Self-Important Critic** — Judges others harshly while overestimating personal insight.
57. **Manipulative Charmer** — Uses likability as a tool for leverage and influence.
58. **Volatile Achiever** — Driven and productive, but emotionally unstable under strain.
59. **Resentful Follower** — Complies outwardly while quietly nursing grievance.
60. **Rigid Perfectionist** — Sets impossible standards and makes flexibility difficult.
61. **Detached Elitist** — Feels above others and avoids genuine emotional connection.
62. **Defensive Skeptic** — Rejects ideas quickly to avoid being vulnerable or wrong.
63. **Impulsive Schemer** — Makes reckless plans without thinking through consequences.
64. **Cowardly Bully** — Pressures weaker people but withdraws when challenged directly.
65. **Jealous Rival** — Measures worth against others and resents their success.
66. **Passive Saboteur** — Avoids open conflict but undermines progress indirectly.
67. **Martyr Complex** — Turns every burden into a performance of personal suffering.
68. **Smug Reformer** — Wants improvement mainly to prove superiority.
69. **Disloyal Survivor** — Switches sides quickly when pressure rises.
70. **Pessimistic Planner** — Expects failure first and often spreads that expectation.
71. **Control-Hungry Caregiver** — Helps others mainly to keep them dependent.
72. **Restless Dissenter** — Rejects plans reflexively and rarely builds alternatives.
73. **Guilt Weaponizer** — Uses shame and obligation to control behavior.
74. **Emotionally Withholding** — Keeps warmth scarce to maintain power or distance.
75. **Petty Scorekeeper** — Tracks every slight and repays them in small ways.
76. **Grandiose Visionary** — Chases sweeping ideas with little grounding or restraint.
77. **Cynical Manipulator** — Assumes everyone is selfish and acts accordingly.
78. **Unstable Loyalist** — Fiercely committed until emotion suddenly flips direction.
79. **Status Addict** — Obsessed with rank, recognition, and public image.
80. **Harsh Pragmatist** — Values results so narrowly that empathy gets discarded.
81. **Catastrophizer** — Turns setbacks into looming disasters in their own mind.
82. **Suspicious Isolator** — Pulls away from others and treats closeness as risk.
83. **Domineering Organizer** — Keeps order through pressure, intrusion, and micromanagement.
84. **Blame Shifter** — Avoids responsibility by redirecting fault onto others.
85. **Moral Showboater** — Performs virtue publicly while behaving inconsistently in private.
86. **Insecure Braggart** — Boasts constantly to cover a fragile self-image.
87. **Compulsive Doubter** — Reopens settled decisions and drains momentum with hesitation.
88. **Predatory Networker** — Builds relationships mainly for extraction and advantage.
89. **Cruel Realist** — Frames unnecessary harshness as honesty.
90. **Envious Imitator** — Copies what works for others while resenting them for it.
91. **Neglectful Dreamer** — Loves ideas but avoids duty, maintenance, and follow-through.
92. **Fearful Conformist** — Follows the group to avoid standing exposed alone.
93. **Vindictive Idealist** — Uses noble language to justify personal retaliation.
94. **Attention Drainer** — Constantly redirects focus back to personal needs and drama.
95. **Reckless Evangelist** — Pushes beliefs aggressively without regard for damage.
96. **Secretly Contemptuous Ally** — Acts supportive while internally scorning everyone involved.
97. **Boundary Breaker** — Ignores limits, privacy, and consent when it suits them.
98. **Tyrannical Protector** — Claims to shield others while controlling them excessively.
99. **Hopeless Defeatist** — Assumes effort is pointless and discourages action around them.
100. **Corrosive Diplomat** — Appears cooperative on the surface while quietly deepening division.
