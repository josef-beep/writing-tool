Initial docked arrangement, illustrated at 1280 × 760. Letters identify notes below; dotted boxes, border grips and generic labels are diagram annotations, not approved product components.

A · Reopen hidden panels at the top

Reopen controls belong at the top of the main window. The single dropdown pictured is a placeholder; menu versus buttons, labels and exact presentation remain open.

B, D, E · Move, resize and hide panels

The starting arrangement places navigation left, context right and timeline below writing. Panels support docking elsewhere and independent floating windows, including on another monitor, plus resizing and collapsing. Exact eligible panels and docking targets remain to define.

Floating panels · Close and monitor recovery

Closing a floating panel hides it until reopened and preserves its content and pending edits. If its saved monitor is unavailable, dock it in the main window. Exact fallback position and later monitor reconnection behavior remain open.

Arrangement · One remembered application layout

Remember panel arrangement across sessions for the whole application and share it across projects, including applicable docked/floating placement, size, collapse and visibility state. This illustration shows no detached window or live interaction.

C · Gray writing area, adjustable display font

The writing background stays gray; its exact shade is open. Choose font family and size from installed fonts and a bundled collection, remembered application-wide. These preferences affect writing-panel display only; saved manuscript formatting and approved export typography keep their existing contracts. No default family, collection, size or fallback is selected here.

D, E · Context and synchronized visibility

Context tabs cover character, faction, plot and decision. The universe timeline exposes maker/category filters, initially all shown. Accepted per-universe filter synchronization and existing save/navigation protections still apply; this diagram does not fabricate a populated context, calendar or filter interaction.
